# BLACK PAGES next source, identity resolution, and review scale

This release adds three operating layers:

1. High-trust external source families beyond Black Chambers / BRW, starting with ByBlack certified Black-owned businesses and curated tourism/food sources as discovery evidence.
2. Candidate identity resolution so the same business discovered from multiple sources becomes one master identity with a multi-source evidence bundle rather than repeated isolated candidates.
3. 25/50/100-candidate staff review batches. Batch evidence decisions advance private candidate verification only; they never auto-publish a directory listing.

Safety rules remain unchanged: discoveries are private, event-only records are excluded, and source membership/certification evidence is reviewable rather than an automatic publication action.
